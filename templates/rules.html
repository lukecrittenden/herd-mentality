Herd Mentality Rules:

Objective:

The aim of the game is to be the first character to collect eight tokens. Try and think like the herd by writing down the same answer as everyone else!

Play:

You will all be asked the same question - keep your answer secret! Once everyone has answered the question, all the responses will be revealed.

Winning Tokens:

Once all the answers are revealed, the Cattle Wrangler will match similar responses into groups. If your answer matches the majority, you will be awarded one token.

The Pink Cow Token:

If your wrote the only unique answer, you receive the Pink Cow Token. This means you can't win the game until you pass it on to another player.
